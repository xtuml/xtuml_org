**Dean** · May 1, 2013 at 3:09 pm

Diagrams are built from objects listed in the palette. There doesn’t appear to be a frame element, but labeled boxes can be constructed from line elements. If you hold down the ctrl key when selecting the line element then a box is drawn by clicking on the 4 corners.
