When working with BridgePoint it is often necessary to have a copy of one or more ooaofooa packages in front of you to help make sense of change you are trying to make to BridgePoint. When you have multiple monitors you may use one monitor for this. However, printouts are also useful, yet many diagrams don’t fit nicely on a page making printing them time consuming. I keep copies of the ooaofooa package diagrams most useful to me on my desktop. I just shared these images. They are found under the public folder: [https://drive.google.com/drive/u/1/folders/0B834tggB4vylR2cxNDRydk91Xzg](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/1/folders/0B834tggB4vylR2cxNDRydk91Xzg) . Please use them, improve them, and add to them as you see fit.
