The xtUML dev team has used Skype for nearly 10 years to communicate on a daily basis. As we moved into the open source world, we wanted to open up this communication to the rest of the world and thus CometChat on xtUML.org was activated. However, after nearly 6 months of usage, we’ve found that CometChat is not meeting our needs.

On the flip side, Skype has recently opened up Skype for Web and public group chats that fill the void that initially drove us to CometChat.

Starting today, we’re disabling CometChat and inviting users and developers to [join the “xtUML Community Chat” conversation inside of Skype](https://join.skype.com/AjKn1exsvwWc). Drop in and ask questions or just watch the chatter!
