**Bob Mulvey** · May 13, 2015 at 3:57 pm

We are well aware that the One Fact team has been publicly quiet over the last several months. This was not because we have been idle :) ! You are now seeing the rollout of things One Fact has worked on. We have been working hard to truly open BridgePoint to this community. Together we will continue to improve, but we believe the tool is now positioned where any engineer with a desire to step in and contribute can do so.

As you may know, the One Fact team has a long history working together with the BridgePoint tool and on xtUML technology. Our team has used skype chat for inner-team communication for many years. We keep rolling chats on many topics. As BridgePoint has moved to the community we understand this communication mechanism is not longer a viable solution. Out “Team Communication” shall be opened to this community.

We are investigating many mechanisms for this for example: slack, hipchat, wordpress chat, cometchat, basecamp, zopim, and others.

We are excited about this move that will allow you vision into what has been our internal communication. Watch for news about this, and please jump in here with any suggestions!

/Bob

**Bob Mulvey** · May 13, 2015 at 7:58 pm

Thanks Travis. Of course notification like that is part of this forum.

The goal is to have a solution that will provide that for those who only want to participate “at a distance”, but also allow more dynamic, and shared communication, like our team has had with skype. Additionally, someone watching at a distance should be able to join in and see the communication chain when they do so. The downside of skype is that the chat (which is really data IMO) is essentially going into a black hole because it is stored in binary format on each participants local machine, and that is no longer acceptable.
