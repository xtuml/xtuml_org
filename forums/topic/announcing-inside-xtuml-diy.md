Inside xtUML DIY is the Do It Yourself guide to creating executable and translatable models. Hosted by members of the xtUML.org community, each podcast episode mixes discussion and step-by-step tutorial to cover a single modeling topic. The series of podcasts begins in April and new episodes will appear each month.
