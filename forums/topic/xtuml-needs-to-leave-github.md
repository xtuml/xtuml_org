**Lee Riemenschneider** · October 10, 2025 at 5:08 pm

In order for xtUML to become a valuable resource in the free software community, it should move away from Microsoft-owned GitHub hosting to a hosting provider that not only is more free and open but also doesn’t steal code without ignoring licensing requirements. Those of us who value our contributions to free and open software development do not want to participate in a closed repository that ignores our wishes.
