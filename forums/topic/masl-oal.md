**cort** · December 7, 2016 at 4:59 pm

BridgePoint now supports multiple action languages.
Right click and Open With-\>OAL Editor to get the right thing here.

Also, be sure to go do Window-\>Preferences-\>xtUML-\>OAL and be sure OAL is selected as your default.
