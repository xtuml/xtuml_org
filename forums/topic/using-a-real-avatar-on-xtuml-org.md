1\) create a [gravatar.com](http://gravatar.com) account (if you don’t already have one)
2) associate the email address you have tied to your xtuml-org.hustlefish-sites.com account to your gravatar account.
a) This will automatically make you have an avatar in the xtuml-org.hustlefish-sites.com forum (and github too).
