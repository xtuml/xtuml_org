Getting started as a BridgePoint developer is easy.  The simple instructions are available in the [BridgePoint Developer’s Getting Started Guide](https://github.com/xtuml/bridgepoint/blob/master/doc-bridgepoint/process/Developer%20Getting%20Started%20Guide.md).

Interested developers are encouraged to contribute to the on-going development of BridgePoint and to contribute xtUML models through the [xtUML github organization](https://github.com/xtuml).

To request features or report bugs, create an account in the [issue tracking system](https://support.onefact.net).  Any additional questions can be posted to the [xtUML forums]({{ '/forum/' | relative_url }}). The xtUML community also maintains [an active google hangouts chat](https://hangouts.google.com/group/WoJOF7gzxSDI16kh2) for users and developers to interact.
