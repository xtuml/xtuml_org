## Training material

One Fact publishes the training material as open source. The courses live in the [xtuml/training](https://github.com/xtuml/training/tree/master/courses/xtUML-Modeling) repository: requirements clarification, and basic xtUML modeling.

Questions about a model belong on the [forum archive]({{ '/forums/xtuml-forum/' | relative_url }}) and, for new conversation, in the [GitHub discussions and issues](https://github.com/xtuml/bridgepoint) of the tool repositories.
