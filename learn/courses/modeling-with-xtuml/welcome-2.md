# Welcome to Modeling with xtUML Editor

The course you are starting will teach you how to use the xtUML Editor. Upon completion, you will be familiar with the features of the Editor and the steps needed to perform common modeling tasks and create models that are executable and translatable.

This course is designed and created as a collaborative effort by members of the xtUML community. We welcome your suggestions, and ask that you please post questions and comments in the community Forum.
