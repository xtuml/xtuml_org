Three public courses. Where a course has lessons, the modules stay in order and each video lesson links to YouTube. The pages are open to anyone.

- [Modeling with xtUML]({{ '/learn/courses/modeling-with-xtuml/' | relative_url }}) introduces modeling with the xtUML Editor and BridgePoint.
- [xtUML Editor Developer Training]({{ '/learn/courses/xtuml-editor-developer-training/' | relative_url }}) covers the tools, source build, and exercises used to change the editor.
- [xtUML Modeling with Realized Components]({{ '/learn/courses/xtuml-modeling-with-realized-components/' | relative_url }}) names four modules. The realized-component videos are in Modeling with xtUML, Module 18.
