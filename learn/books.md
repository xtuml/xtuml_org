The following books can be valuable in learning more about model-driven development and design:

Mellor, Stephen J., and Marc J. Balcer. *Executable UML: A Foundation for Model-driven Architecture*. Boston: Addison-Wesley, 2002. Print.

Mellor, Stephen J. *MDA Distilled: Principles of Model-driven Architecture*. Boston: Addison-Wesley, 2004. Print.

Starr, Leon. *Executable UML: How to Build Class Models*. Upper Saddle River, NJ: Prentice Hall, 2002. Print.

Raistrick, Francis, Wright, Carter, Wilke.  *Model Driven Architecture with Executable UML*. Cambridge UP, 2004.

Ambler, Scott W. *The Elements of UML 2.0 Style*. Cambridge UP, 2005. Print.
